about=Network optimization toolkit. Generate vector layers of various shapes (circle, sector, sword, etc.) from an Excel table containing longitude, latitude, azimuth and size fields, without any fixed template. Export layers to SHP, TAB, KML and KMZ. Run containment queries to find which small layers are located inside a boundary layer, based on start-point coordinates or area percentage. Buffer or shrink point, line and polygon layers by a given distance, including single-sided expansion for line layers. Analyze co-sited and co-covered networks: find co-sited data by coordinate range, and identify co-coverage by the overlap ratio between two sectors or drawn shapes, useful for 3CC composition planning and 2G/3G/4G/5G co-sited and co-covered evaluation.
